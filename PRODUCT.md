# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and potential collaborators evaluating Jiu Axl Tabilla (UI/UX designer + frontend/full-stack developer) for roles or project work. They land here from a resume link or social profile, often skimming quickly on a laptop or phone between other candidates/portfolios. Their job to be done: form a fast impression of design/technical skill, then verify it against real experience, projects, and design work before reaching out.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio built as a playful pastiche of a Google Search Results Page: the nav is a search bar, "About Me" / "Projects" / "Designs" / "Socials" are search-style result lists, there's a "People Also Ask" FAQ accordion and a "Knowledge Panel" bio card, right down to a fake "did you mean" 404. The joke is the concept, not a disguise: it exists to make the portfolio itself a demonstration of UI/UX craft and attention to detail, while still surfacing real bio, experience, projects, and contact info clearly and fast. Success looks like: a reviewer smiles at the concept within the first few seconds, then finds what they came for (skills, experience, project links, contact) without the joke getting in the way.

## Brand Personality

Precise, clever, credible. Playful and obviously a pastiche, not a deadpan forgery, the fun is in how well-executed and self-aware the joke is, not in fooling anyone into thinking it's a real Google page. Confidence expressed through craft (the SERP details are genuinely well made) rather than through hype or self-promotion copy.

## Anti-references

Generic AI/template portfolio look: glassy SaaS cards, gradient hero sections, glassmorphism-by-default, identical icon-card grids, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows, numbered 01/02/03 section scaffolding, card-hover-lift clichés. Also avoid drifting into an actual Google trademark/font forgery (e.g. sourcing "Google Sans"/"Product Sans") purely to look more "authentic" — the pastiche should read as loving and clearly original, not as an attempt to impersonate Google's real brand assets.

## Design Principles

- The joke should be obviously a wink, not a forgery: playful exaggeration over pixel-perfect deception.
- Every SERP element earns its place as real portfolio content (the metaphor organizes information, it doesn't just decorate).
- The craft of the pastiche IS the resume: execution quality (motion, spacing, contrast, detail) is the actual demonstration of skill.
- Real content stays fast to find: a recruiter skimming quickly should never lose the bio/projects/contact under the bit.
- Accessibility and polish are craft, not a compliance checkbox: reduced motion, contrast, and keyboard support are part of what makes the joke land well.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG 2.1 AA: ≥4.5:1 body text contrast, ≥3:1 large text, full keyboard navigation with visible focus states, and `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks (crossfade/instant) for every added animation. No additional unusual requirements specified.
